None of this makes a limited edition a bad purchase. A coloured pressing from a small label can be a way to back a record and the people who made it. But a fifth variant of an album you already own is not the same as discovering and supporting new music. Before the next pre-order, it is worth asking which of the two you are paying for.

M Sources & Methods

Where every number comes from, what the words mean, and what this data cannot tell you.

Data

    All figures are computed in the notebook 02_analysis.ipynb and exported as JSON; this site only reads those files.

    Definitions

    Limited
    The release’s format descriptions include “Limited Edition”.
    States a quantity
    The notebook found a pressing number (e.g. “limited to 500”) in the release’s Notes field.
    Design
    Format descriptions + free-text description, normalised. One design pressed for two regions = two releases.
    Look / sound words
    Look = picture disc, or a colour/effect word (black excluded). Sound = 120–200 g / heavyweight, remaster, audiophile, half-speed.
    Want
    Number of Discogs users with the release on their wantlist.
    95% interval
    Bootstrap over versions (5,000 resamples).

    Limits

    • Discogs is catalogued by its users. A missing quantity or word may be missing from the listing, not from the record.
    • Discogs users are not all buyers; wants and owners reflect an active collecting community.
    • The opening and chapter 01 use Discogs API counts (27 Sep 2026); other shares across all releases use the data dump (1 Oct 2026). The two differ slightly.
    • Chapter 02 stickers: one per vinyl listing of boygenius, the record (2023), typeset in one shared style after the real hype stickers, not photographs. Text from each listing: “Limited Edition” and the colour from its format; “Retailer exclusive” and the barcode only where a contributor wrote down the sticker text. Quantity under each sticker: from the listing’s notes (boygenius_listings.json).
    • Fig 03c (Olivia Rodrigo): not Discogs data. Streaming and vinyl runtimes and the removed passages as timed by a fan on Reddit (r/OliviaRodrigo), transcribed into olivia_vinyl_runtimes_reddit_2026-10-12.csv. Totals are summed from the songs; the post’s own totals differ by 2 seconds (rounding).
    • Fig 01b: the bar’s total (all limited releases) = the top 20 labels’ limited releases ÷ their share (labels.json → top20_share_pct).
    • Pop (chapter 01): yearly Discogs counts of Pop vinyl releases, total and labelled “Limited Edition” (collected 27 Sep 2026, file discogs_pop_vinyl_2026-09-27.csv), from only.
    • Want, tracklist and resale comparisons (chapters 02, 03, 05) come from a smaller API sample of albums with both limited and standard versions.
    • Nothing here measures sound quality, buyers’ motives or the size of pressing runs.
