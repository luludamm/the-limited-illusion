Research question In the streaming era, has the vinyl revival’s promise of material authenticity and scarcity turned into an overused marketing strategy?

More colours. More editions. More reasons to pre-order. We looked at indie rock vinyl releases listed on Discogs to find out how common the ‘Limited Edition’ label has become—and what the listings can actually tell us about scarcity.

    M Notes

    Sources & Methods

    Where the numbers come from, and what they cannot tell you.

    Data

    The main analysis uses Discogs vinyl release records for indie rock from 1990 to 2025, with pop vinyl (2015–2025) included for comparison. The data combines the Discogs data dump of 1 October 2026 and an API collection made between 27 September and 11 October 2026.

    The analysis was conducted in Python, and the website displays the resulting figures. The Olivia Rodrigo vinyl-versus-streaming comparison is a separate example based on song timings recorded by a fan on Reddit; it is not part of the main Discogs dataset.

    Figures may differ slightly where the API collection and data dump cover different records or use different collection dates.

    Images

    Album covers and product photos found through Google Images; fan photos from Instagram; record photos from Pinterest. Fan and record photos open their original post.

    What the data can’t tell you

    • Pressing quantity is not measured for every release. “Limited” counts the word on a listing, not the size of a run.
    • A missing pressing quantity does not prove that information was intentionally withheld. Discogs is written by its users; a number may simply not be entered.
    • Matching titles or tracklists do not establish that two releases use identical audio.
    • Wantlist activity indicates interest, not confirmed purchases or resale value.
    • Release descriptions reflect what users or labels record on Discogs, not every physical feature of a record.
    • The dataset does not establish why labels choose particular release strategies or how much revenue those strategies generate.
